## 1. What this covers
OYUN lists the Partner's venue(s) on its website so players can reserve and pay an advance for slots. The venue stays under the Partner's control. OYUN is a booking platform, not the operator of the venue.

## 2. Listing and accuracy
- The Partner gives true details: venue name, address, photos, sports, prices, opening hours, amenities, and keeps them current in the Partner dashboard.
- The Partner holds every licence and permission needed to run the venue (trade licence, fire and safety, music or event permissions, insurance) and is responsible for the condition and safety of the venue and equipment. `[LAWYER: confirm liability wording]`
- OYUN may remove or suspend a listing that is inaccurate, unsafe, or receives repeated complaints, after telling the Partner where practical.

## 3. Bookings
- A booking is confirmed when the player sees a booking code and QR code. The Partner honours every confirmed booking at the booked time and price.
- Players pay an advance to OYUN online. The rest is paid at the venue (cash or UPI), and the Partner records it at check-in.
- The Partner checks players in with the code. Slots the Partner blocks stop being sold; blocking a slot that already has a confirmed booking is not allowed without cancelling it through the dashboard, which refunds the player.

- The Partner is responsible for its own GST, income tax and other taxes on its income.
- Payouts can be put on hold while a dispute, a chargeback or a suspected fraud is investigated.

## 5. Cancellations and refunds
- Players may cancel up to 2 hours before the slot for a 100% refund of the advance to their OYUN wallet. The Partner is not paid for a booking cancelled this way.
- If the Partner cancels, cannot host the slot, or the venue is unplayable (including the monsoon guarantee for open outdoor turfs), the player receives a 100% refund and the Partner is not paid for that booking. Repeated Partner cancellations may lead to suspension.

## 6. Reviews and conduct
Players may rate venues. The Partner will not post or ask for fake reviews, and will treat players lawfully and respectfully.

## 7. Personal data
Player name and phone number are shared with the Partner only to run the booking. The Partner uses them for nothing else, does not sell or share them, deletes them when no longer needed, and follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. See the DPDP brief for the open questions about roles (fiduciary or processor).
